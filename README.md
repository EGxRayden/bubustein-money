## 🚀 What makes this mod special?

> **Ever dreamed of a realistic economy in Minecraft?** Your search ends here! Bubustein's Money Mod brings **28 real-world currencies**, advanced banking systems, and immersive economic gameplay to your world.

### ✨ **Key Features**

🏦 **Advanced Banking System**
- ATMs for easy money management (Not available yet WIP)
- Secure card-based transactions using commands (`/bubustein help`)
- Multiple card types with different tax rates

💱 **Professional Trading**
- Banker villagers with exclusive trades
- Currency exchange services
- Regional currencies in village houses
- 🔥 Want netherite without touching lava? 💸 Buy it. Want enchanted gear without grinding XP for hours? 💱Trade it. With the right currency in hand, the Banker🏛️ becomes your market plug for **OP** loot.

🌍 **28 Global Currencies**
- From USD and EUR to exotic currencies like ISK and PHP
- Realistic banknotes and coins
- Historical currencies (ROL 1900-1906)

🏛️ **Custom Structures**
- Luxurious mansions with valuable loot
- Desert castles and jungle huts
- Regional bank buildings
